# QuickCommerce Intelligence: Business Insights

## 1. Project Overview

This analysis examines sales performance, product categories, delivery operations, refunds, and customer ratings across Blinkit, JioMart, and Swiggy Instamart.

The findings below are based on the metrics displayed in the Power BI dashboard.

## 2. Sales Performance

### Revenue and Orders

* Reported total revenue: ₹59.10 million.
* Reported total orders: 100,000.
* Reported average order value: ₹590.99.

**Business interpretation:** These metrics provide a high-level view of sales performance. Tracking them over time can help identify changes in order volume, customer spending, and revenue.

### Product Category Performance

* Personal Care has the highest displayed category revenue at approximately ₹17 million.
* Grocery follows at approximately ₹14 million.
* Snacks has the lowest displayed category revenue at approximately ₹5 million.

**Business interpretation:** Personal Care and Grocery are important categories to investigate when planning product availability and promotional strategies. Further analysis is needed to determine the reasons for differences in category performance.

### Platform Revenue Contribution

The dashboard displays revenue contributions of approximately ₹20 million per platform, with each platform contributing around one-third of the total.

**Business interpretation:** The displayed figures suggest a relatively balanced revenue distribution across the three platforms. This should be verified against the underlying data and exact measures.

## 3. Delivery Performance

* Reported average delivery time: 29.54 minutes.
* Reported delayed orders: approximately 14,000.
* The dashboard indicates that approximately 86% of orders were delivered without a recorded delay.

**Business interpretation:** Delivery time and delay frequency are useful operational indicators. Further analysis by location, time period, and platform could help identify where delivery performance needs improvement.

## 4. Customer Experience

### Customer Ratings

* Overall average rating: 3.24.
* JioMart: approximately 3.25.
* Swiggy Instamart: approximately 3.24.
* Blinkit: approximately 3.23.

**Business interpretation:** The displayed average ratings are similar across platforms. More detailed analysis of rating distributions and customer feedback would help identify the main drivers of satisfaction.

### Refund Requests

* The displayed refund rate is 45.82%.

**Business interpretation:** This figure warrants further investigation. Before making business recommendations, validate the refund-rate calculation, its denominator, and the underlying refund records.

## 5. Recommendations

1. Monitor revenue and order trends over time.
2. Investigate the drivers of category-level revenue differences.
3. Analyze delivery delays by platform and other available operational dimensions.
4. Validate refund calculations and examine common refund reasons.
5. Monitor customer ratings and investigate opportunities to improve satisfaction.
6. Review KPI definitions to ensure that metrics are calculated consistently across platforms.

## 6. Limitations

* Findings depend on the quality and coverage of the available dataset.
* Displayed figures are rounded and should be validated against the underlying data.
* The dashboard alone does not establish the causes of revenue differences, delivery delays, or refunds.
* Business recommendations should be tested with further analysis before implementation.

## Conclusion

QuickCommerce Intelligence demonstrates how Excel and Power BI can be used to organize sales data, visualize key performance indicators, compare platforms, and identify areas for further business investigation.
